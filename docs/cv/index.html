Research Profile

I am a senior undergraduate researcher at Southeast University, working on multimodal foundation models, agentic AI, and embodied intelligence. My research focuses on how AI systems acquire, organize, and generalize knowledge, reason over complex environments, and translate reasoning into reliable real-world actions.

Building generalizable AI systems that reason, learn, and act in the physical world.

Publications

Knowledge Externalization: Reversible Unlearning and Modular Retrieval in Multimodal Large Language Models

Jiaqi Li, Zihan You, Ruoyan Shen, Shenyu Zhang, Songlin Zhai, Yongrui Chen, Chuanyi Zhang, Jiahui Geng, Fakhri Karray, Sheng Bi, Guilin Qi

ICLR 2026 · Peer-reviewed · Co-First Author

Knowledge Externalization moves targeted parametric knowledge into modular memory tokens so a multimodal model can forget, recover, and compose that knowledge. Dual-Stream Memory fine-tuning and MEXBench study this post-training setting, including gradient interference when several concepts are externalized together.

MambaBEV: A BEV-Based 3D Detection Model with Mamba2

Zihan You, Ni Wang, Hao Wang, Qichao Zhao, Jinxiang Wang

ICPR 2026 · Peer-reviewed · First Author

MambaBEV is a BEV 3D detector with a Mamba2 temporal fusion module for global context. On nuScenes, the paper reports 51.7% NDS and 42.7% mAP for MambaBEV-base.

Hybrid Guided Variational Autoencoder for Visual Place Recognition

Ni Wang, Zihan You, Emre Neftci, Thorben Schoepe

ICPR 2026 · Peer-reviewed · Second Author

This paper studies a hybrid guided variational autoencoder for visual place recognition. The authors release the code and the Aachen-Indoor-VPR event and RGB dataset with the paper.

Language2Drive: Do Vision-Language-Action Models Really Follow the Instructions in Autonomous Driving?

Zihan You*†, Zehua Fan*, Wenqi Lyu*, Aoqi Wang*, Ni Wang, Yifei Yang, Linge Zhao, Huan Li, Sining Ang, Yuguang Yang, Jueyu Chen, Xiangyu Li, Qianshan Wei, Jieyuan Pei, Yuxin Wang, Zhiwen Chen, Jiaqi Li, Wenxuan Song, Yanyuan Qiao, Benjin Zhu, Haoang Li, Yan Wang‡

* Core contributor† Project lead‡ Corresponding author

arXiv 2026 · Preprint · First Author · Project Lead

Language2Drive tests whether a natural-language instruction changes what a driving vision-language-action model does. Its paired counterfactual unit keeps the scene fixed and changes only the instruction. Across the six principal CARLA systems, 93.2% of successful individual instruction executions have no successful counterpart under the alternative feasible instruction.

World4Scorer: Outcome-Grounded World Modeling for Autonomous Driving

Jieyuan Pei*‡, Meiyi Lu*, Sining Ang, Yubo Zhao, Zhangyi Hu, Mingwei Xu, Haokai Ding, Wei Li, Zihan You, Jianwei Zheng, Li Yu, Yifeng Pan, Ji Tao, Rongjunchen Zhang, Yan Wang†

* Equal contribution† Corresponding author‡ Work done during an internship

arXiv 2026 · Preprint · Co-Author

World4Scorer builds a driving scorer as a trajectory-conditioned predictor. Simulator outcomes supervise every candidate plan, and the observed future of the executed trajectory anchors the shared predictor during training. I am a co-author.

MobileWAM: Bridging World Action Models to Mobile Manipulation with Chain-of-Foresight

Zehua Fan*, Junjie He*, Wenxuan Song*, Xi Wang, Wenqi Lyu, Linge Zhao, Fuhao Li, Zihan You, Yifei Yang, Kaiming Xu, Qi Jiang, Yue Jiang, Haoang Li, Cheng Chi†, Feng Gao, Bailin Li†, Yan Wang†

* Equal contribution† Corresponding author

arXiv 2026 · Preprint · Co-Author

MobileWAM adapts a video world-action model to mobile manipulation. The action expert mixes shared, locomotion, and manipulation pathways, and Chain-of-Foresight adds training-only future-latent supervision. The manuscript reports a 73.0% mean success rate on ManiSkill-HAB and transfer to a real ARX Lift2 robot. Code has not been released.

SAMoE-VLA: A Scene Adaptive Mixture-of-Experts Vision-Language-Action Model for Autonomous Driving

Zihan You, Hongwei Liu, Chenxu Dang, Zhe Wang, Sining Ang, Aoqi Wang, Yan Wang*

* Corresponding author

arXiv 2026 · Preprint · First Author

SAMoE-VLA studies scene-level mixture-of-experts routing for continuous driving actions, using bird's-eye-view features rather than token-level expert selection. The public repository currently contains the project overview and figures, not a full training implementation.

Dual-Priv Pruning : Efficient Differential Private Fine-Tuning in Multimodal Large Language Models

Qianshan Wei*, Jiaqi Li*, Zihan You*, Yi Zhan, Kecen Li, Jialin Wu, Xinfeng Li, Hengjun Liu, Yi Yu, Bin Cao, Yiwen Xu, Yang Liu, Guilin Qi†

* Equal contribution† Corresponding author

arXiv 2025 · Preprint · Co-First Author

Dual-Priv Pruning combines visual-token pruning and gradient-update pruning for differentially private fine-tuning of multimodal large language models, together with low-rank adaptation and Rényi differential privacy accounting.

SparseOccVLA: Bridging Occupancy and Vision-Language Models via Sparse Queries for Unified 4D Scene Understanding and Planning

Chenxu Dang*, Jie Wang, Guang Li, Zhiwen Hou, Zihan You, Hangjun Ye, Jie Ma, Long Chen†, Yan Wang†

* Work done during an internship at Xiaomi EV and AIR† Corresponding author

arXiv 2026 · Preprint · Co-Author

SparseOccVLA connects semantic occupancy and vision-language models through sparse queries for 4D scene understanding and planning. I am a co-author on the paper.

Engineering and Robotics

The same hardware work is part of why I study agents that have to produce physical artifacts. Agents' Last Exam asks whether a foundation-model agent can carry a natural-language requirement through design tools, fabrication, integration, and an independent physical check.

The benchmark is still being designed. Nothing on this page is a result from a completed large-scale physical evaluation, and there is no public task set or leaderboard yet.

Agents' Last Exam on the research page

Electronics and PCB

Circuit and board-level work from RoboMaster and related hardware projects, including power management and embedded integration.

Passive supercapacitor balancing board

Designed a passive balancing board for series supercapacitors used in the robot power system.

Two-switch buck–boost power supply

Designed the circuit and PCB for a two-switch buck–boost supply for the robot platform.

Rechargeable handheld armor plate

Built a rechargeable handheld armor plate, including the circuit, PCB, and embedded integration.

Mechanical Design

Structural design in SOLIDWORKS, including mobile-robot mechanics and a harvesting machine, with the mechanical and electrical design done together.

Mobile robot mechanical systems

Designed mechanical structures for mobile robots and fit them to the electronics and the rest of the assembly.

Chinese-chive harvesting and bundling machine

Worked on an integrated machine that harvests and bundles Chinese chives, covering the mechanical structure and the electrical co-design.

Robotics

Competition robots, from motion control and system integration through debugging and functional checks. ROS and ROS 2 are part of this workflow.

RoboMaster development

Worked on motion control, hardware–software integration, system debugging, and functional validation for RoboMaster robots.

Robot software and simulation

Use ROS and ROS 2 with the embedded platforms, and use simulation when checking behavior before running it on the robot.

Simulation and Physical AI

Simulators used in the driving research, plus hardware-in-the-loop evaluation as an ongoing direction of Agents' Last Exam.

CARLA and AlpaSim

Language2Drive's closed-loop tracks run in CARLA, including six purpose-built maps, and in reconstructed scenes through AlpaSim.

MuJoCo and Isaac Sim

MuJoCo and Isaac Sim are part of the simulation toolkit I use for robot and embodied-AI experiments.

Hardware-in-the-loop evaluation

Hardware-in-the-loop testing is part of the ongoing Agents' Last Exam benchmark design. It is not a completed evaluation campaign.
